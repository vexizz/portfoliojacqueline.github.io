Fintech onboarding

0 to 1 Product DesignMobile app12 weeksShipped

Rethinking identity verification so new customers could open an account in minutes, cutting sign-up drop-off by 34% without loosening compliance.

Role
Lead Product Designer
Team
1 PM, 4 engineers, 1 researcher
Timeline
12 weeks · 2025
Tools
Figma, Maze, Amplitude
01

Overview The challenge

Almost half of the people who downloaded the app never finished signing up. The culprit was identity verification: a long, unexplained wall of document uploads right at the start.

My brief was to make onboarding feel quick and trustworthy while still meeting every regulatory requirement.

02

Research Finding where people gave up

I paired funnel data with 12 user interviews and a review of 400 support tickets to see not just where people dropped off, but why.

  • Mapped the full sign-up funnel in Amplitude and found the two biggest drop-off points
  • Ran 12 remote interviews with people who had abandoned sign-up
  • Tagged and clustered 400 support tickets into 6 themes
  • Audited 8 competitor onboarding flows
03

Design Explaining before asking

The key insight: people didn't mind verifying, they minded not knowing why or how long it would take. So the new flow lets people explore first, then explains each step before asking for anything.

  • Sketched 30+ concepts and narrowed to 3 directions with the team
  • Designed a "browse first, verify later" flow approved by compliance
  • Built a smart camera screen that catches blurry photos before upload
  • Prototyped every state, including errors and edge cases
I thought this would take ages. It was done before my coffee was. Participant, usability test round 2
04

Test & ship Two rounds, one launch

Two rounds of unmoderated testing on Maze helped refine copy and the camera guidance. We then launched behind a feature flag to 20% of new users before rolling out to everyone.

05

Results What changed

Three months after launch:

−34%Sign-up drop-off
2.1×Faster verification
4.7★App store rating
06

Reflection What I learned

Bringing compliance into the room early turned them from blockers into collaborators. Next time I'd do that from day one, and I'd invest earlier in tracking how people recover from errors.
