One-page checkout

E-commerceA/B tested8 weeksShipped

Folding a five-step checkout into a single, calm page for a home-goods retailer, lifting conversion by 12% in the first month.

Role
Product Designer
Team
1 PM, 3 engineers, 1 analyst
Timeline
8 weeks · 2024
Tools
Figma, Hotjar, Optimizely
01

Overview The challenge

7 in 10 shoppers who reached checkout didn't finish. Each of the five steps reloaded the page, surprise delivery costs appeared at the very end, and account creation was mandatory.

02

Discover Watching people check out

Session recordings and heatmaps showed the same story again and again: people hesitating at delivery costs and bouncing at the "create account" wall.

  • Reviewed 150 Hotjar session recordings and tagged friction moments
  • Mapped the existing flow step by step with every error state
  • Benchmarked 10 best-in-class checkouts
03

Design Everything on one page

The new checkout shows the full cost from the start, lets people check out as a guest, and reveals each section as the last one is completed, so it feels short without hiding anything.

  • Designed a progressive single-page layout with inline validation
  • Moved delivery costs into the basket so there are no surprises
  • Added guest checkout and Apple Pay / Google Pay express options
  • Wrote all microcopy and error messages
04

Results A/B tested, then shipped

We ran a four-week A/B test against the old checkout before rolling the new one out to everyone.

+12%Checkout conversion
−38%Time to complete
41%Used guest checkout
05

Reflection What I learned

Small honesty fixes, like showing costs early, did as much as the big layout change. I'd test those quick wins on their own first next time.
